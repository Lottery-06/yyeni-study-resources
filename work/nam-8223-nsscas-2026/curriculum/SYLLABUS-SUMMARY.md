# NSSCAS Biology 8223 — syllabus summary (for verification)

Source: NIED syllabus, signed off 26 Nov 2020, first exam 2021, 50 pp. Extracted text read in full. Annexes B–D read 2026-10-18 (see below). Corrected 2026-10-18 after an independent Codex review of the first draft; each correction was re-checked against the syllabus text.

## Qualification
- One-year course, about 180 guided learning hours. NSSCO Biology is recommended prior learning, not a stated entry requirement. Grades a–e.
- **Paper 1:** 40 multiple-choice items, 1 h, 40 marks (AO A and B).
- **Paper 2:** short-answer, structured and free-response questions, 1 h 15, 60 marks (AO A and B).
- **Paper 3:** advanced practical, 2 h, 40 marks (AO C only). The syllabus says candidates "collect, record and analyse data" (p. 22) and Annexe A covers calculation, data presentation, deductions, conclusions and evaluation. Flashcards cannot test hands-on manipulation, but can test those data and evaluation skills.
- AO A knowledge with understanding 40% (not more than 20% recall); AO B handling information and solving problems 40%; AO C practical 20% — as printed in the grid.
- The syllabus itself says "60% skills vs 40% knowledge", so recall-only cards cover a minority of what is examined.
- The glossary (section 10, p. 25) gives command-word meanings as a guide that is "neither exhaustive nor definitive"; a term's meaning depends partly on context. Using them consistently in cards is our design choice, not a syllabus requirement for flashcards.

## Content: 3 themes, 25 leaf units (YYeni has 3 + 18 + 4 corresponding lessons; some titles are semantic rather than verbatim matches, so confirm the mapping per lesson)
**Theme 1 Classification of living organisms (3):** 1.1 Classification; 1.2 Biodiversity (Simpson's index, Lincoln index, sampling); 1.3 Conservation.

**Theme 2 Organisation and maintenance of the organism (18):**
2.1 Microscope; 2.2 Cell structure; 2.3.1 Fluid mosaic membranes; 2.3.2 Movement into and out of cells; 2.4.1 Carbohydrates and lipids; 2.4.2 Proteins and water; 2.5.1 Mode of enzyme action; 2.5.2 Factors affecting enzyme action (Vmax, Km, inhibitors, immobilisation); 2.6.1 Structure of transport tissues; 2.6.2 Transport mechanisms in plants; 2.7.1 Mammalian circulatory system (blood, tissue fluid, Hb, O2 dissociation curve, Bohr shift); 2.7.2 Mammalian heart; 2.8.1 Infectious disease (cholera, malaria, TB, HIV/AIDS, measles); 2.8.2 Antibiotics; 2.9.1 Immune response; 2.9.2 Antibodies and vaccination; 2.10.1 Gas exchange system; 2.10.2 Smoking.

**Theme 3 Development of the organism and the continuity of life (4):** 3.1.1 Replication and division of nuclei and cells; 3.1.2 Chromosome behaviour in mitosis; 3.2.1 Structure and replication of DNA; 3.2.2 Protein synthesis.

## Boundaries stated in the syllabus (flashcards must respect these)
- No calculations involving water potential (2.3.2 and 2.6.2). Solute potential is not expected (stated in 2.3.2 only).
- Chloride shift details not required (2.7.1).
- Malarial life cycle in detail not required (2.8.1).
- Heart control: SAN, AVN and Purkyne tissue only, no nervous or hormonal control (2.7.2).
- Structural formulae of nucleotides and bases not required (3.2.1).
- No reference to mesosomes (2.2). Only Simpson's Index of Diversity is stated as "provided". The syllabus points to the Lincoln index "as shown in the Mathematical requirements", but Annexe C contains no Lincoln formula and does not say it will be supplied. Treat the Lincoln formula as something learners may need to know until a source says otherwise.
- Non-infectious disease examples limited to sickle cell anaemia and lung cancer (2.8.1).
- Other stated limits: species concepts limited to biological, morphological, ecological and behavioural (1.1); sampling methods limited to frame quadrats, line and belt transects and mark-release-recapture (1.2); habitat restoration limited to local or regional examples (1.3); roles of water limited to solvent action, specific heat capacity and latent heat of vaporisation (2.4.2); T-lymphocytes limited to T-helper and T-killer cells (2.9.1); chromosome structure limited to DNA, histone proteins, chromatids, centromere and telomeres (3.1.1). Check the syllabus for any other "limited to" wording before writing a card.

## Problems in the syllabus text itself
1. **Paper weighting: three conflicting figures.** Prose: Papers 1+2 = 77%, Paper 3 = 23%. Table: 29% / 42% / 29%. Marks (40/60/40 of 140): 28.6% / 42.9% / 28.6%. The table is close to the marks but not equal to them.
2. **AO weighting conflicts with the grid's marks.** Printed 40% A / 40% B / 20% C, but the grid's marks (A 20+30 = 50, B 20+30 = 50, C 40, of 140) give 35.7% / 35.7% / 28.6%.
3. **Practical objectives are not "not carded".** Cards cannot assess physical manipulation, but they can test experimental planning, variables, data processing, errors, conclusions and evaluation. The plan should card those aspects and leave only the hands-on skill uncarded.
4. Lesson 2.3 and the 2.4–2.10 headings group two lessons each, but the YYeni lesson titles drop the numbers and some are not verbatim, so map by content and confirm per lesson.

## Annexes (read 2026-10-18)
- **Annexe C (mathematical requirements, p. 43) matters for cards:** averages, decimals, fractions, percentages, ratios and reciprocals, standard notation, use of ruler and compasses, translating information between graphical, numerical and algebraic forms, joining points with straight ruled lines versus a line (straight or curved) of best fit, direct and inverse proportion, indices, magnification and actual size, significant figures, percentages and percentage change, percentage error, ratios, rate of change from a graph, tangent slope, chart and graph choice and scales, frequency distributions, SI prefixes (giga to nano), length/surface-area/volume relationships, geometry (areas, circumferences, volumes), spreadsheet use, Simpson's index.
- **Annexe B (printed p. 39; the syllabus contents page wrongly lists it as p. 37):** Paper 3 apparatus; microscopes have a x10 eyepiece, x10 and x40 objectives and an eyepiece graticule; DNEA/Regional office supplies stage micrometer scales "as needed".
- **Annexe D (pp. 44-46):** numbers and SI units (singular), conventions for tables, graphs, pie and bar charts, column graphs and histograms, plus taxonomy and further terminology lists that examiners use when setting papers. Cards should follow these conventions.
