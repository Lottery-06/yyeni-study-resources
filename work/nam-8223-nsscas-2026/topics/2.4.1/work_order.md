**CU-8223-2.4.1** — sub-topic 2.4.1, 12 objectives, 3600–5160 words
- `OBJ-8223-2.4.1-01` tier 1 — define the terms monomer, polymer, macromolecule, monosaccharide, disaccharide and polysaccharide

- `OBJ-8223-2.4.1-03` tier 2 — describe the formation of a glycosidic bond by condensation,with reference both to polysaccharides and to disaccharides, including sucrose
- `OBJ-8223-2.4.1-04` tier 2 — describe the breakage of glycosidic bonds in polysaccharides and disaccharides by hydrolysis, with reference to the non-reducing sugar test
- `OBJ-8223-2.4.1-05` tier 2 — describe the molecular structure of the polysaccharides starch (amylose and amylopectin) and glycogen and relate their structures to their functions in living organisms
- `OBJ-8223-2.4.1-06` tier 2 — describe the molecular structure of the polysaccharide cellulose and outline how the arrangement of cellulose molecules contributes to the function of plant cell walls

- `OBJ-8223-2.4.1-08` tier 2 — describe the structure of a phospholipid and relate the structure to the functions of phospholipids in living organisms

- `OBJ-8223-2.4.1-12` tier 2 — carry out a semi-quantitative Benedict's test on a reducing sugar using dilution, standardising the test and using the results (colour standards or time to first colour change) to estimate the concentration
