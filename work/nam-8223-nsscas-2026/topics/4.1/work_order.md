## Performance tasks

- **practical_task** — A Paper 3 style investigation task (about 10 marks): an experiment described in words with our own readings in a table. The learner decides variables, controls and how to measure, draws up the results table (quantity and unit headings, consistent significant figures), plots or describes the graph, calculates a value with working, identifies sources of error and suggests an improvement. Readings are ours and are labelled as such.
- **practical_task** — A second Paper 3 style task (about 10 marks), different in setting from the first and fitted to this topic. Where the topic concerns microscopy, drawing or measuring specimens: a specimen or photomicrograph described in words with measurements given, asking what a plan diagram or cell drawing would show, a graticule calibration or actual-size or magnification calculation with working in micrometres, a comparison of two specimens by observable features only, and an improvement. Otherwise: a second, different investigation (a different experiment, data set or results table) testing the same skills as this topic's objectives. Values and readings are ours and are labelled as such.
