## Limits of the mining itself
- Mappings were made by reading, five agents by exam year, with confidence recorded per record (high, medium, low). 35 records are low confidence. Six 2025 Paper 1 items are placeholders (the report names no topic) and are excluded from exposure counts; seven more are ambiguous.
- Diagram, graph and micrograph pages were not all viewed (2023 P1 Q4, Q27, Q38; 2024 P2 and P3 micrograph and figure pages; 2022 P2 Q3 images and P3 images; the 2025 report's figure pages). Those records say so in `notes`. They need a page-image check before any card is written from them.
- Spot checks done by the lead: all 120 available Paper 1 keys against the reports (0 mismatches); 14 randomly chosen mappings read by meaning (all sensible); facility figures for 2024 and 2025 matched. Not done: a full second-reader audit of the 404 mappings.
- Part tariffs reconcile to the printed question totals for 2022, 2023 and 2024, and 2021 Paper 2. They do not for 2021 Paper 3 (42 against 40). 2025 tariffs are mostly unstated (31 of 38 Paper 2 and 3 parts), so 2025 contributes no tariff statistics.
- "AO focus" tags are our judgement of what each part demands, not a board statement.
- The register of source doubts in the corpus analysis lists places where the board's printed key or scheme looks biologically doubtful. Those are readings by the agents or by us, not board statements, and are not yet verified against a textbook.
