## Findings that shape the plan
1. **Much of what is examined is application, not recall.** Writing anticodons, reading tRNA sequences, naming a mitosis stage from a micrograph, and "suggest how..." questions can be carded only partly. Pure recall (definitions, enzyme names and functions, telomere description, RNA vs DNA, transcription outline) fits cards; sequence tasks fit parametrised practice items and image cards; "suggest" questions fit explain/evaluate cards with a model chain.
2. **Needs images:** mitosis stage recognition and chromosome-structure labelling cannot be tested in text-only cards. YYeni supports managed images; sourcing diagrams is a plan decision.
3. **Mutation is in the exams more than the lesson list suggests:** 2024 Q3(d) and 2022 Q40 test gene mutation effects under protein synthesis. The syllabus covers it at 3.2.2 (substitution, deletion, insertion; HbA/HbS).
4. **Open for the plan:** answers must come from the syllabus and standard biology, verified independently, since no board scheme exists. Mark-bearing "expected answers" above are marked unconfirmed.

**Report-wide guidance that should shape card wording (all subjects share the language points; Biology specifics are marked):**
- Recall was answered fairly well; application, calculation and graph interpretation poorly. "Emphasise application and interpretation, not rote memorisation."
- Biology: candidates described a graph instead of explaining it; bullet points are not acceptable for describe, explain, suggest, discuss or outline; spelling of biological terms (e.g. Casparian strip) cost marks; "PH" is wrong, write "pH"; time, distance and size words: increase/decrease, not higher/lower/greater.
- Mark schemes use numbered marking points with max caps, "ecf" (error carried forward), A (accept) and R (reject) lists. A card's answer for a describe/explain question should be written as marking points, and its marking guidance should carry the reject list.
- Teachers are told to mark strictly and give no benefit-of-the-doubt marks, consistent with the Recall Test rubric that the card is the mark scheme.
