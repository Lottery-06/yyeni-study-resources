## Deep Research adjudication (2026-10-18; review/DEEP-RESEARCH-RESULT-2026-10-18.pdf)
Secondary-source evidence only (many citations are Scribd, tutorial or revision sites; none is a person's sign-off). Claims stay `unverified`; a Biology person still has to approve.
- **CO / haemoglobin:** syllabus 2.7.1-08 itself says CO reduces the affinity of haemoglobin for oxygen (the report omitted that clause). Notes now use competitive binding plus the syllabus phrase; the fuller allosteric left-shift is true but beyond the syllabus and not taught.
- **Simpson's index:** report confirms D = 1 - sum(n/N)^2 and that the syllabus form is a misprint; also says the n(n-1) variant is Edexcel's. No change. Optional: add a card on the 1 - D double-subtraction error.
- **Lysosomes:** report says UK boards reject lysosomes on plant diagrams; evidence thin, and plant lysosome-like compartments exist. Notes changed to the exam convention (lysosomes taught as animal-cell organelles; vacuole does the lytic job in plants), not to 'plants have none'.
- **Measles / maternal antibodies:** placental IgG is the main systemic protection; breast-milk IgA is mucosal; ~95% threshold from 1 - 1/R0 with R0 about 15, WHO position paper. Our claims say placenta and breast milk are both natural passive and 95%; no error found.
